## 🎨 Design System

### **Typography**
- **Display**: Playfair Display (elegant, serif)
- **Body**: Inter (clean, modern)
- **Sizes**: 
  - Hero: 6xl - 9xl (96px - 128px)
  - Headings: 4xl - 7xl (36px - 72px)
  - Body: lg - xl (18px - 20px)

### **Colors**
- **Background**: White (#FFFFFF)
- **Text**: Black (#000000)
- **Gray Scale**: 100, 200, 400, 500, 600 for accents
- **Minimal color usage**: Focus on typography & whitespace

### **Spacing**
- Large sections: py-32 (8rem vertical padding)
- Medium gaps: gap-8 (2rem)
- Small gaps: gap-4 (1rem)
- Generous whitespace throughout

### **Animations**
- Fade in: 1s ease-out
- Slide up: 0.8s ease-out
- Stagger delays: 0.05s - 0.1s per item
- Hover effects: 700ms transform
- All transitions smooth and subtle
